# Florida Invest Launch

Build a simple, clean, minimal one-page website for "Florida Invest", a financial education and guidance brand for adults 45+.

Details:
- Brand: Florida Invest
- Target audience: Adults 45+ seeking straightforward guidance on saving, planning, and investing
- Address: 15807 Biscayne Blvd #101, North Miami Beach, FL 33160, United States
- Phone: +1 (305) 206-5720 (clickable tel:+13052065720)

Style & Aesthetic:
- Minimal, premium financial-services aesthetic: white/light background, dark navy/charcoal typography, subtle calm accent color (like deep slate blue/navy), plenty of whitespace, clean typography, large readable text.
- No flashy elements, tickers, hype, fake testimonials, popups, or aggressive sales talk. Highly compliant for Google/Meta ads.

Structure:
1. Header: Logo "Florida Invest", navigation links (How We Help, About, Contact), prominent "CALL NOW" button (tel:+13052065720).
2. Hero: Headline "Make More Informed Decisions About Your Money", supporting copy on straightforward guidance, prominent primary CTA "CALL NOW" button with subtext "Speak with our team to learn more."
3. How We Help: Headline "Simple Guidance. Clearer Financial Decisions." with 3 simple educational cards: "Save Smarter", "Understand Your Options", "Plan With Greater Clarity".
4. About Florida Invest: Headline "Guidance You Can Understand", copy explaining focus on adults 45+ with no confusing jargon or unrealistic promises, paired with a subtle, tasteful image.
5. CTA Section: Full-width clean section with headline "Have Questions About Your Financial Options?", text, and large button "CALL NOW — (305) 206-5720".
6. Contact Section: Business name, full address, phone number, embedded map or styled location card, and CALL NOW button.
7. Footer: Brand, address, phone, links for Privacy Policy, Terms & Conditions, Disclaimer modals/pages, and the conservative financial disclaimer provided.

SEO:
- Title: Florida Invest | Financial Guidance & Investment Education
- Meta description: Florida Invest provides straightforward guidance on saving, investing, and financial planning for adults 45+. Call our team to learn more.
- Clean semantic HTML structure, responsive, mobile-first.
